The Official Book
SHINE: A Decade of Murals

Celebrate ten years of the internationally recognized SHINE Mural Festival with this premium book documenting the murals, artists, and community impact that helped transform St. Petersburg, Florida into a global destination for public art.

A collector's edition publication featuring more than 250 pages of full-color photography, artist profiles, behind-the-scenes stories, and reflections from muralists and community leaders. Designed as both a visual centerpiece and a historical archive, it captures a decade of creativity, collaboration, and cultural legacy through large-scale public art.
