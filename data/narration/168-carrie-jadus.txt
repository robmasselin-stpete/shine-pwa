That oversized hundred-dollar bill in front of you swaps Benjamin Franklin for a pair of hands cradling a curled-up newborn. Carrie Jadus started it in twenty fifteen as a study for The Motherhood Project, then blew it up into this wall in twenty seventeen for the ArtsXchange grand opening. What the fetus and the currency have to do with each other, she leaves entirely up to you.

Jadus has a degree in electrical engineering. She designed radio-frequency circuits for years, then quit to paint full time. Now she turns out two to three hundred pieces a year, at every scale from notecards to fifty-foot walls.

Look closely at the details. She copied real currency faithfully — the corners, the scrollwork, the teal and blue-green. But check the signature line. Where a Treasurer's name should be, it reads, "Are You My Mother?"
