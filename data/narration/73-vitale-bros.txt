That paper napkin thing is real. The first mural started as a sketch on a restaurant napkin, handed to the owner, back in nineteen ninety-two. Thirty-plus years later, this is where it lands: a full row of SpongeBob characters, painted at building scale.

Johnny and his brother Paul started out hand-lettering signs and painting storefront windows around Tampa Bay. No formal training. Just three generations of artists in the family. They've painted for the Rays, the Bucs, and L'Oreal, and they call themselves the mural police, restoring damaged walls around the city for free.

They've painted SHINE every single year since it began.

Look at the outlines. Clean, confident, no wobble. That's what thirty years of practice buys you. And yes, this is the wall everybody stops to photograph.
