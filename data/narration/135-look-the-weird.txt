What reads as color and calm from across the street turns unsettling up close — melting landscapes, rippling glass, plant tendrils that don't quite behave. That's the point. Lars Wunderlich, who paints as LOOK the Weird, built this piece around Florida's environment: hurricane damage, red tide, the contradictions of living here. He says he loves to provoke questions.

He's been painting walls since he was fifteen, in East Germany, and he co-founded an international crew called The Weird. These days his work leans into algorithms and glitches — portraits generated by machine, then distorted by hand.
