When Isac Gres left Cuba in twenty nineteen, he walked through twelve countries to reach the United States — including the Darién Gap — and he brought his brushes. This is the tallest wall he's ever painted.

It wraps the corner of the Melting Pot, and Gres liked the name. A melting pot of cultures, rendered in swirling cosmic surrealism. Molten planets, blazing nebula clouds, deep space blues against fiery orange and gold.

The melting candles are his nod to Dalí, the surrealist who made clocks droop.

Gres trained in Cuba in cubism, hyperrealism, and surrealism, ran a tattoo studio in Uruguay, then settled here in St. Petersburg. Light drives everything he makes. "Color for me means light," he says.

Look for the city skyline sealed inside a dripping glass orb, and the elephant emerging from the fire.
