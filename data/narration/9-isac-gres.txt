When EEsac Gress left Cuba in twenty nineteen, he walked through twelve countries to reach the United States — including the darien gap — and he brought his brushes. This is the tallest wall he's ever painted.
[]
It wraps the corner of the Melting Pot, and Gress liked the name. A melting pot of cultures, rendered in swirling cosmic surrealism. Molten planets, blazing nebula clouds, deep space blues against fiery orange and gold.
[]
The melting candles are his nod to Dali, the surrealist who made clocks droop.

Gress trained in Cuba in cubism, hyperrealism, and surrealism, ran a tattoo studio in Uruguay, then settled here in St. Petersburg. Light drives everything he makes. "Color for me means light," he says. Look for the city skyline sealed inside a dripping glass orb, and the elephant emerging from the fire.
