Look closely at those sunglasses. The lenses aren't just reflective — they're mirroring a waterfront skyline right back at you. That's the whole trick of the piece, hidden in plain sight.

Melanie Posner paints women like this on purpose: strong, confident, the kind you're meant to be inspired by. Oversized eyes, vibrant color, and here, a scatter of polka dots in pink, gold, red, and light blue against cool teal.

She earned her painting degree at the University of Tampa, and now works out of Los Angeles. Her range is wide. She's done scenic art for Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge at Disney World, and an eighteen-foot mural on the back of the Hollander Hotel that she finished in under five days.
