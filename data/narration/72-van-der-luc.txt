That polar bear hauling a tattered robot across the grass? That's the artist talking about the environment. No lectures, no melting ice caps — just a white bear on the move with a passenger strapped to its back.

Lucas Franco signs his work Van Der Luc. He's self-taught, born in Bogota, Colombia, now painting out of Pinellas Park. His subjects are huge — climate, technology, nature — but the delivery is pure storybook.

He painted this for SHINE in twenty twenty-two, the same year he became a full festival mural artist.
