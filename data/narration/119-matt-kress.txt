Air traffic controllers have a name for the plane this artist painted. They call it "Skittles." Matt Kress covered a nineteen sixty-seven Mooney in forty-five colors, and it won Best Unique Paint Scheme at Oshkosh.

That same instinct for bold color is right in front of you. These are pets, painted like stained glass — a Boston terrier, a big dark dog, a tabby cat, all broken into faceted blocks of blue, orange, green and red against a kaleidoscope of diamonds.

Kress spent almost sixteen years in a desk job, afraid art wasn't a real career. He visited St. Pete in twenty fourteen, saw a town full of murals, and moved. His first commission paid a thousand dollars for two weeks of work. He's since painted more than thirty walls, including a seventy-foot sea turtle in Tampa.

Look at the tabby's whiskers.
