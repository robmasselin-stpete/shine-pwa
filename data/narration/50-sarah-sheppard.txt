Look at those eyes. They hold you before you notice everything around them — the golden mask, the crescent moons, the starburst crown, the white wings spreading wide across the wall.

That gold filigree scrollwork isn't a coincidence. Sarah Sheppard trained as a metalsmith, taking up work her late father began. Both her parents were artists. She's lived in St. Petersburg for over twelve years, and she's got murals all over this city, including one on the back wall of the Morayan Arts Center, commissioned by AARP.

She painted this one for SHINE, in twenty twenty-three. Her thing is movement as metaphor — continuous loss and seeking.

The whole piece works like a gilded altar, symmetrical and mystical, but reimagined for a wall on Fifth Avenue South.

Reed the script across the lower center, if you haven't already.   We are all magic.
