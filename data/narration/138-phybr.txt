Everything on this wall is falling toward a single point, and if you stare too long, you go with it. It's a vortex of childhood — Tom and Jerry mid-chase, a Rubik's cube, a Game Boy, a Tamagotchi, and a pink Furby with glowing blue eyes, all tumbling into a dark center. Look close: every toy casts its own shadow, catching light as if it's really spinning through space.

The artist is Ken Dushane the Third, who paints as PHYBR. He's put up more than two hundred and fifty murals in twelve years, worked for Universal Pictures, and — briefly — spent two weeks as A$AP Rocky's creative director. He also has a background in horticulture, which explains the tropical plants in his other work.

Find the grey Beanie Baby elephant. Its little red heart tag is still attached.
