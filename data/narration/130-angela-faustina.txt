Nine days, one brush. That's how Angela Faustina painted the two walls of giant citrus you're standing between here at Green Bench Brewing. No roller, no spray can — brush only. At the time, it was the largest work she'd ever made.

Look close at the slices. Blood oranges, grapefruits, lemons, limes, navel oranges, all cut clean through and blown up to a scale you'd never see in your kitchen — the membrane, the seeds, the juice. Faustina works in hyperrealistic oil, magnifying what lives just under the peel.

She lives with lupus, and the work carries that: vivid color and delicate intricacy, strength and fragility in the same frame.

The left wall runs warm, heavy on blood orange and grapefruit. The right cools off with lemon and lime. Find the blood orange on the left — that deep magenta is her showing you what's hiding inside the skin.
