The gold letters cascading down this hot pink wall aren't decoration. They're a mantra, written in Devanagari script, the same sacred text repeated across the entire wall like a visual chant.  Look closely at the gold clusters. Underneath them, red and pink swirls move like a current behind the words.

The artist goes by Imagine. Her name is Sneha Shrestha, born in Nepal, based in Boston. She holds a master's from Harvard, where she works at the Mittal South Asia Institute. She founded Nepal's first Children's Art Museum. And she is the first contemporary Nepali artist in the collection of the Museum of Fine Arts Boston.

Her style fuses Buddhist mandala traditions with graffiti and American abstract expressionism. She paints these mantras using a disciplined breathing technique, one letter at a time.

She made this for SHINE in twenty twenty-two.
