One of her illustrations traveled to two hundred thirty-eight cities in sixty-nine countries — chosen for the global Creative Mornings theme "Rise." The artist is Miss Crit, and she's from right here.

Laura Spencer was born and raised in St. Petersburg, trained at Gibbs High School's arts center, then earned an illustration degree from Ringling. She describes her own style as a little bit spooky and a little bit psychedelic. She co-created a fifty-thousand-dollar mosaic at Sunset Park, and she leads SHINE's trolley tour of the murals.

This one reads "Nowness is the Portal," the cream lettering curving through a jungle of monstera, ti plants, and broad tropical leaves against deep teal. The words are the invitation. The garden is the reward.

Look, where a single white magnolia bloom is opening — the one bright flower in all that green.
