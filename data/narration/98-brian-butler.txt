The man who painted this has drawn more than two thousand live concerts from the crowd — sketching bands in real time, from the audience, in a series he calls "Show Drawn." Brian Butler works out of Miami, trained at the Massachusetts College of Art and Design, and his usual subject is music. His pen has landed at SXSW, Bonnaroo, and Governors Ball, and his clients run from the Miami Heat to Wu-Tang Clan.

Here he's gone quieter. This wraps the whole building in tropical foliage and birds in flight, one continuous green canopy across every face of the wall. Bold outlines, soft color underneath — that hand-drawn, storybook look is his signature.

Follow the linework where it turns a corner. The plants don't stop at the edge of the building. They keep growing around it.
