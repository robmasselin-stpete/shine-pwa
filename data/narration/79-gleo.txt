The faces in front of you were made without a single spray can. Gleo works in acrylics — brushes and rollers, the slow way. That patience once carried her across a fifty-thousand-square-foot grain elevator in Wichita, Kansas, which set the record for the largest acrylic mural painted by one artist.

She's Nathalia Gallego Sanchez, born in Cali, Colombia, in nineteen ninety-two. Her name is wordplay pulled from her surname, Gallego. She studied chemistry by day, art by night, and decided the formal training was too theoretical. So she went her own way.

Her work pulls from Colombian folklore and ancestral cosmology — women with patterned faces, flowers woven through their hair. She painted this for SHINE in twenty twenty-one.

Look at how the faces overlap and gaze in different directions, and find the sunflowers tucked between them, blooming in the gaps.
