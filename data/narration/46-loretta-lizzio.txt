Those three women watching you? They're sirens — the ones from Greek myth who lured sailors to the rocks with a song too beautiful to resist. Look closely and you'll see they aren't just standing in the foliage. Their skin is threaded with leaf patterns and fine line work, so they seem to grow straight out of the plants. Beautiful and a little sinister, all at once.

Loretta Lizzio painted this. She came all the way from the Gold Coast of Australia for SHINE in twenty twenty-three. She was the first woman commissioned to paint Melbourne's Hellenic Museum — three fearless women from Greek history and myth. Sirens, clearly, are a returning theme.

Now follow their dark hair up. It flows and tangles together across the top of the wall, forming a kind of canopy over all three. One song, three voices.
