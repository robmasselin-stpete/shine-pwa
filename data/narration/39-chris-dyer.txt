That spaceship is made entirely of creatures — tentacles, multiple eyes, geometric patterns, all interlocking into a single vessel blasting across the wall on rainbow flames and lightning. Chris Dyer painted it for SHINE in twenty twenty-three.

Dyer was born in Ottawa, moved to Leema at four, and came back to Canada at seventeen. That mix runs through everything here — Incan and pre-Columbian imagery, sacred geometry, and nineteen-eighties skate culture. He's designed over a hundred skateboard graphics for Santa Cruz and Deathwish, painted in forty-five countries, and has a mural in the Bob Marley Museum. He founded his brand, Positive Creations — the words are floating right above the ship. These days he lives in a purple house in Gulfport, and his murals are all over the area.

Look for the pilot in the blue bubble cockpit, flashing a peace sign. Then find the smiling sun face in the upper right corner... Definitly. Classic Dyer.  One of my favorites from twenty twenty three
