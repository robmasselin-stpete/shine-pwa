That tattooed figure glaring sideways at you? The word across its chest reads "blooms" — and it's not the flower kind. I-boms painted this for the Sea Walls program, which sends artists after ocean threats. His target here is red tide, the harmful algal bloom that turns Florida's coast toxic and lines the beaches with dead fish.

I-bombs is a St. Petersburg native. The name stands for "illegal. Beauty. On. My. Streets." His solo show at Mize Gallery sold out in forty-eight hours. He works by what he calls the iceberg theory — a bright, celebratory surface with something heavier underneath.

You are standing in front of the surface right now. Look low, at the base of the wall, past those piled brown cloud shapes. There's a thin wavy white line, and a few small fish swimming along the bottom edge. That's where the bloom begins.
