That cowboy on the right isn't riding a horse. Look again — he's swinging a lasso from the back of a green alligator, palm trees and a sunset behind him. Classic Americana postcard, run through a Florida filter.

This is the work of the Vitale Bros, Johnny and Paul, out of Tampa Bay. No formal training between them — just a family line of artists going back to their grandparents, and thirty-plus years of practice. Their very first mural started as a sketch on a paper napkin, handed to a restaurant owner back in nineteen ninety-two.

Before you move on, look at the frame itself. The wood grain, the nail heads — all painted.
