Count them if you want. Good luck. There are hundreds of hand-drawn icons packed edge to edge on this teal wall, and no two are the same. Flamingos, an octopus, a UFO, pizza slices, a Volkswagen bus, an all-seeing eye. It's Florida and pop culture crammed together with no space wasted.

Chad Mize has been doing tongue-in-cheek work like this around St. Pete since two thousand three. He co-founded the Bluelucy gallery on Central Avenue, later opened his own gallery, and spent a decade helping organize the Shine mural festival. You may already own his handiwork, that "Paris, Tokyo, London, St. Pete" shirt that's been selling in local shops for over fifteen years.

Look closely and you'll notice two real windows and a white door built right into the collage. And up in the corner, the trademark signature. Mize.
