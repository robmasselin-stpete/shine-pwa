The artist who painted these three flamingos started making murals for a practical reason. Her San Francisco apartments were too small to hold a studio. So she painted outside, on whatever wall she could find, walked away, and treated each one like a modern-day cave painting.

Bunnie Reiss now lives on a five-acre desert homestead near Joshua Tree, where she grows food and keeps chickens. Her patterns come from Russian fairy tales, Mexican folk art, and Jewish mysticism, and she's carried them onto walls in more than twenty countries.

Look closely at the flamingos. From a distance they read as pink birds on long dark legs. Up close, each one is packed with tiny flowers and geometric shapes, no two the same. Follow their feet down to the blue-violet rainbow they're stepping over, and the golden blooms on the right, against that teal sky.  

One of my favorites, the mural so much more impressive from the alley than on central avenue.
