Five stories of interlocking color, and you can see it from streets away. This is Up and Over — a collaboration between Tes One and the Vitale Brothers, three artists whose paths run through Tampa Bay walls going back to nineteen ninety-two.

Tes One started tagging at fourteen, came up through the arts program at Gibbs High School, and went on to paint for Nike, Toyota, and the Brooklyn Nets. He also co-founded the SHINE Mural Festival, the reason so much of this city is covered in work like this. The Vitale Brothers bring figurative realism and pop sensibility. Together, the style is his bold graphic design fused with their instinct for depth.

Look at how the circles sit nested inside those flowing vertical bands. The colors are doing the work here — reds against teals against deep blue — pulling the whole facade forward and back at once.
