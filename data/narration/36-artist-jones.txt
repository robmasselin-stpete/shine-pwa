Every surface you're looking at is painted — every corner, every utility box, the whole PSTA ticket office wrapped like a single quilt. Artist Jones covered all of it for SHINE twenty twenty-three. The patchwork comes from African textile traditions, stitched together in blocks that collide on purpose. Oversized fan-shaped leaves in hot pink and sky blue. Navy spirals. Lime green tiger stripes. Follow one pattern around a corner and watch it change into something else. Jones was born in Trinidad and Tobago, and those Caribbean tones run all through her work. She's based in Tampa now, with a degree in graphic design and more than ten years working as a designer before she turned to painting full time. Painting runs in the family — her great-great grandmother worked as a painter as well.  

On one side of the mural there is a tribute to John Donaldson.  Donaldson was one of the first pioneers to this area, purchasing 40 acres that would be the beginning of St Petersburg.
