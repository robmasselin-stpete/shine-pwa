Every surface you're looking at is painted — every corner, every utility box, the whole PSTA ticket office wrapped like a single quilt. Artist Jones covered all of it for SHINE twenty twenty-three. The patchwork comes from African textile traditions, stitched together in blocks that collide on purpose. Oversized fan-shaped leaves in hot pink and sky blue. Navy spirals. Lime green tiger stripes. Follow one pattern around a corner and watch it change into something else. Nothing here stops where the wall bends. Jones was born in Trinidad and Tobago, and those Caribbean tones run all through her work. She's based in Tampa now, with a degree in graphic design and more than ten years working as a designer before she turned to painting full time. Painting runs in the family — her great-great grandmother worked as a painter as well.
