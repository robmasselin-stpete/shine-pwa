An American alligator, snout to tail, running the length of an entire building. The head alone eats up about a third of the wall. Look at the scale texturing in those rich browns and chocolates, then the golden sunflowers crowding in behind it against a teal sky. It's a Florida postcard, blown up to the size of a building.

This is the work of The Happy Mural Project — Alyssa Marie and Braden Everly, a married couple who moved here from Colorado. Their motto is "Honk if You're Happy." Their first mural went up days before the world shut down in the spring of twenty twenty.

Oversized sunflowers are their signature, and Florida wildlife runs through much of their work. They painted this one, "Wild Ride," as three-time SHINE artists.

Follow the tail all the way to the far end. It doesn't quit.
