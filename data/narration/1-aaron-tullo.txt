The Milwaukee Brewers drafted this artist twice. He picked up a paintbrush instead.

Aaron Tullo was born here in St. Petersburg in nineteen eighty-eight and grew up throwing fastballs. He turned the Brewers down once for college, then signed the second time, out of the University of Tennessee. He pitched in their minor league system for about six years before injuries ended it. He was around twenty-five.

The pivot to painting took. He learned the trade with the Vitale Brothers in Gulfport, and he still letters every sign by hand.

What you're looking at is five stories of white peacock, painted for SHINE Origins in twenty twenty-five, on the Cordova Inn — the festival's title sponsor.

The plumage runs the full vertical height of the building. It's built to read from blocks away, so let your eye follow the feathers all the way to the top.
