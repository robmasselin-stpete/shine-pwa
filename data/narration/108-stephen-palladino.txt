Tiffany hires this man. So does Lady Gaga. Stephen Palladino was painting large-scale murals across New York City as a teenager, and here he's given ninety-five feet of wall to fill.

From where you're standing, it reads as a cheery abstract puzzle — aqua, orange, lime green, purple. Step closer. The bright color breaks apart into bold black line drawings, faces and distorted figures in a style rooted in German Expressionism. Slick and gritty at the same time.

Palladino was born in the Bronx in nineteen eighty-six. Classical art training at age ten, but the real education happened on the streets.

Look for the words scrawled across the wall, in his own handwriting. "Love me." "Nobody but you." "Not true." Find the red woman in profile, and the man in the blue suit. He painted all of it for SHINE, in twenty seventeen.
