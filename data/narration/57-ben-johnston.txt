Once, Ben Johnston covered nearly sixteen thousand square feet of wall for a brewery using nothing but Sharpies and a scaffold. So the precision you're looking at here is earned.

Four letters. R, I, S, E. Built to look like solid three-dimensional blocks floating off the wall, in graduating shades of cerulean, sky blue, and white. It runs the full height of the building. You caught it from blocks away, which is the point.

Johnston grew up in Cape Town, taught himself, and now works out of Toronto. His clients include Nike, Adobe, and Apple, where he also gives talks. He built this one for SHINE, back in twenty twenty-two.

He often picks his words with the local community. Keep going. Together. Rise...

Look at the shadows under the letters. That crisp edge is what sells the illusion that the whole word is hovering over Central Avenue.
