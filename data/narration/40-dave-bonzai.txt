Those letters look like they were poured in molten metal, still dripping. They weren't. There's no chrome paint in the world. Dave Bonzai builds that effect with black, white, and grey alone, layered until the eye gives up and calls it liquid metal. He named the technique "Liquid Chrome," and one of his early pieces sits in the permanent collection of the STRAAT Museum in Amsterdam.

He's from Southampton, England, based in London, and has been working the streets for over twenty years. His clients include Triumph Motorcycles. He painted this one for SHINE in twenty twenty-three, and called it a modern piece with a retro twist.

Look at the sky behind the script — deep purple bleeding into hot pink and fiery red, palm trees in silhouette, sharp diagonal lines cutting the black. Pure synthwave sunset. Now look at the chrome again, and try to find the paint.
