This wall was Jenipher Chandley's first festival mural — SHINE, twenty twenty-one. Before this, she painted on canvas. Now she's got giant lemons glowing against navy blue on the side of a tile shop.

Chandley was born and raised right here in St. Petersburg. She works mostly in oils — figurative, a little surreal — and her one recurring subject is the story of love: seeking it, losing it, finding it again. This wall is lighter. Just citrus, orange blossoms, and a small pep talk hiding in the design.

Back around twenty eleven she started painting on nights outside a bar on Central. Today she curates that same building and hosts a free art meetup every Wednesday.
