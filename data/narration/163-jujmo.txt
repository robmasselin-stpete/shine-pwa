Those blob creatures peeking out at you with dot eyes and little mouths? Blame anime. The artist, Cheryl Weber, who signs her work Jujmo, grew up a military kid moving between bases overseas, with almost no access to cartoons. When she finally got to watch anime in the States, she decided to become an artist.

She's Filipina American, based in Tampa, with a fine arts degree from the University of South Florida and more than ten years of murals behind her. In twenty twenty, she painted the "B" in the Black Lives Matter street mural at the Dr. Carter G. Woodson Museum.

Look closely and you'll notice there isn't a single straight line anywhere here. Everything curves and intertwines — coral, amoebas, all in soft pinks and deep blues, drawn from Japanese and Filipino folklore. An underwater garden that never quite decides what planet it's on...
