That egret spans several stories, and every petal, every feather, was laid down in spray paint. No brushes. The artist behind it is Taylor Smith, who goes by Dreamweaver. She painted her first mural at seven, on her aunt's backyard shed in Waco, Texas, and hasn't stopped since — something like a hundred and fifty walls, close to twenty of them here in St. Pete.

She works almost entirely from a can, blending soft-focus realism with pure color. Nature runs through all of it: birds, water, flowers. This one she painted for SHINE Origins in twenty twenty-five, the festival's return to the block where the whole movement began back in twenty twelve.

Follow the egret's neck as it rises up the left wall, threading through the blooms. The plumage is delicate. The petals are the size of doors.  Amazing work by Dreamweaver!
