Growing up on military bases overseas, Cheryl Weber had almost no access to cartoons. When her family moved back to the States, anime hit her like a freight train. She decided, on the spot, to become an artist.

She works under the name Jujmo, and this is what she calls "cute forward" — saturated color, zero straight lines, blob creatures and smiley-faced amoebas piled on top of each other like a candy-colored coral reef. She earned her fine arts degree here in Tampa Bay and went freelance the day after graduation.

You may know her pastels. This is warmer — orange, teal, gold, brown. Look for the little smiling faces peeking out of the organic shapes, just dot eyes and a wide grin, tucked between the flowers and sparkles.
