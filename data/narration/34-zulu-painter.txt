These four faces are people from this community, everyone in some way touched by family turmoil. Zulu Painter said that plainly, and he painted them across two stories of the CASA Family Justice Center, a place for survivors of domestic violence.

Look at the woman cradling a baby. Inside the curls of her hair are words — drive, bravery, family, growth, hope, empathy, love, community, wisdom, faith, create, unity. On the other side, a young woman reaches out a helping hand, and a young man holds your gaze directly.

The artist, Carlos Culbertson, calls these his Mindful Murals. A four-time SHINE artist and a Muse Award winner, he works from a studio here in St. Pete.
