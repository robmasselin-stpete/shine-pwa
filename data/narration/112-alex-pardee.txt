That melting rainbow where the creature's head should be? Alex Pardee calls monsters like this one his "Brightmares" — nightmares in impossibly bright colors. He's been chasing that contradiction since childhood, weaned on Garbage Pail Kids and Tales From the Crypt, and entirely self-taught.

You've seen his work without knowing it. He designed creatures for Zack Snyder's Sucker Punch, and album art for The Used. Here, for SHINE twenty sixteen, he went bigger than he ever had — forty-six feet wide, with help from fellow artist Ricky Watts.

The whole thing looks like it's surrendering to the Florida heat. That deflating orb where a face should be, the colors running straight down the wall, the skeletal gray figure hunched underneath it.
