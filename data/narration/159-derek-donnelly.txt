The whole wall is gray, silver-blue, and starlight — except for two small sunsets, one in each lens of the boy's sunglasses. That warm orange-gold is the only color Derek Donnelly allows himself here. Look at the lenses.

Donnelly is a Pinellas County native who once painted a T-Rex in a Hawaiian shirt going fishing, for AARP. That tells you most of what you need to know. He graduated from the arts program at Gibbs High School, studied illustration in Tampa, and went full-time on murals in twenty ten. His son turns up in his work all over the city.
