Everything on this wall is hand-painted, and none of it is old. It just looks like it's been here since nineteen fifty-five.

Jeff Williams is a St. Petersburg native, and he taught himself this trade in twenty twelve after watching a documentary called "Sign Painters." Before that he was a leather engraver, then a house painter — making his own work at night with whatever paint was left over. His shop, Sun Signs and Lettering, runs the way one would have run in nineteen fifty-five. His hand is on the signage at Sunken Gardens, and he lettered a truck for Disney Springs.

What you're looking at is a stack of vintage Florida tourism signs — souvenirs, oranges, pin-ups, the whole postcard fantasy...
