Quinn Cale builds her paintings to animate — you scan the wall, and the chase you're looking at comes alive: this leaping cat, that pixelated orange mouse, and a retro cursor hand, all of it running across the brick. Cale is self-taught. No art degree. She started painting on wood slats as a teenager because she couldn't afford to go out with friends. Later she worked at Apple, first in sales, then as a Creative teaching kids to make digital art on iPads. That's where the moving pictures come from. Look at the fur. It's broken into bold blocks of purple, teal, orange, and pink — a neon stained-glass window in the shape of a cat, mid-pounce, against the dark teal behind it. She painted this awesome mural for Shine twenty twenty-four.
