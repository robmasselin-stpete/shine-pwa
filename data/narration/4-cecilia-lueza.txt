She once painted an entire intersection with fifty volunteers. A storm washed it away, so they painted it again.

That's the kind of persistence behind the hummingbird in front of you. Cecilia Lueza calls this one "Radiance," made for SHINE twenty twenty-five. The bird hovers mid-flight, wings spread, in iridescent greens, blues, and oranges, against a field of overlapping translucent circles — her signature geometry.

Lueza was born in subtropical northeast Argentina, all dense flora and fauna, and it still runs through her work. She started out painting in monochrome. Florida's light changed that.

Since twenty thirteen she's made nearly sixty public installations — the Atlanta BeltLine, the North Carolina Museum of Art, a kinetic angel in Chattanooga.

Look at the wings up close. The color shifts as the light moves, the way it does on a real hummingbird.  Check out her other murals here in St Pete, they are just as amazing
