Yes, that's a T-Rex in a Hawaiian shirt going fishing. And yes, it was painted for AARP.

Derek Donnelly made this one in twenty eighteen as part of a series called "Age Friendly St. Pete." The dinosaur, fedora and round glasses and fishing rod over the bay, stands in for the older generation of this city enjoying life to the fullest. The joke lands, but it means something.

Donnelly is a Pinellas County native who went full-time on murals back in twenty ten. He builds things for other people, too. When his mentor Bill Correira died, Donnelly organized a forty-artist tribute mural on Central Avenue that's still a landmark.

Look at the T-Rex's shirt, blue and white, and follow his line out over the water. Behind him, a pink-and-blue sunset frames the skyline across the bay.
