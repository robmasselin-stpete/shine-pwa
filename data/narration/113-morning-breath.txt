That pamphlet in the sinister figure's hand? It's real. In nineteen seventy-five, New York City police handed out a survival guide called "Welcome to Fear City," warning tourists to stay off the streets after dark. Morning Breath painted the whole thing — a hundred and one feet of it — to look like a vintage horror comic held open wide. The nods to New York are everywhere, a tribute to the Coney Island Sandwich Shop, slinging food here since nineteen twenty-six.

Behind it are Doug Cunningham and Jason Noto, a Brooklyn studio that has designed over a hundred album covers for Foo Fighters, Jay-Z, Slayer. They won a Grammy for Best Recording Package in twenty twenty-one.

Look to the far right, above the wall. That red devil with the curling mustache and horns is grinning down at you.
