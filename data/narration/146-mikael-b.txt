The crystals erupting from the blue sea — that's the artist himself. He titled this wall "Inner Visions" and said the whole point was simple: keep dreams alive.

Mikael B started tagging the streets of Copenhagen at fifteen under the name KETS. Now he paints for Nike and Bentley, shows alongside Banksy and Kaws, and runs a foundation that builds schools in Cambodia. He made this for the SHINE festival in twenty seventeen — the same year he moved fully into abstraction.

Look at how the wall splits. Cool icy blues on the left, warm sunset pinks and corals on the right, with sharp faceted crystal shards colliding into sweeping curves. The graphic designer in him is underneath all of it — hard geometry meeting fluid energy.

Along the bottom edge, find the misty teal clouds. That's the turbulent sea the crystals are erupting from.
