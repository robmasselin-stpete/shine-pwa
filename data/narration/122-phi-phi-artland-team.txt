Every one of these faces was painted by a different pair of hands. Neighbors, families, first-time painters — this wall came together over four weekends in twenty twenty-four, each weekend led by a different artist from the Phi Phi Artland team.

The project started small, as a contest to give one local storefront a free mural. Then Hurricanes Helene and Milton hit Florida, and Sara Saylem, who led the whole thing, wanted more than a pretty wall. She wanted people making something together. A grant from the St. Pete Arts Alliance made it happen.

So look at how the faces work. Each one is a block of bold color — greens, purples, pinks, oranges — overlapping and interlocking like puzzle pieces. No single face stands alone.

And read the gold script on the right. Stronger together. They mean the painting itself...
