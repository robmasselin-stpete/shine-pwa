The woman looking back at you is Iryna Zarutska. She fled Ukraine during the Russian invasion and resettled in Charlotte, North Carolina. On the twenty-second of August, twenty twenty-five, she was stabbed from behind while riding the light rail. She was twenty-three. Her death drew international attention, and North Carolina passed a law in her name, reforming bail and mental health evaluations.

Melanie Posner painted her. Posner is known for photorealistic portraits of women, usually in bold, saturated color. Here she did the opposite. She pulled everything back to a soft gradient, mint green on one side, warm gold on the other, and let Iryna's face carry the wall.
