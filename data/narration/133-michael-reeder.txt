Reed this wall left to right and you're watching a life story run backwards. It starts as pure pattern — pink, teal, and gold cubes that seem to float off the bricks, right across the roll-up garage door. Then a photorealistic human skull pushes through the illusion. And at the far right, a living face — just a nose, eyes, and brow in warm flesh tones — peeks out from behind the bone. Abstraction, death, life. That's the shell, and what's beneath it.

Michael Reeder built his name mixing that kind of lifelike detail with flat graphic space. He earned his painting degree in New York, did a residency at Red Bull House of Art in Detroit, and painted this for SHINE in twenty sixteen.
