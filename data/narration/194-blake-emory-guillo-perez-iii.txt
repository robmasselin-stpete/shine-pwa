Those tentacles aren't staying on the wall. Look at the corner — the octopus body sits flat on the concrete, but its arms peel forward, climbing past the wall plane and reaching out into open air. Part mural, part sculpture. Saturated color against raw industrial block.

It's a twenty twenty-three collaboration between Blake Emory and painter Guillo Perez the Third, wrapping the corner of Cage Brewing.

Emory doesn't pick a lane. Neo-Renaissance figure painting, optic modernism, sculpture, set design, experimental film — all of it. In twenty twenty-five the city unveiled his life-size bronze of Babe Ruth at the called-shot moment, down on the Pier.

His murals turn up in places you don't expect around St. Pete, including a few rooftops. You find them by looking up.

So look up. Follow one tentacle to the point where it leaves the wall entirely.  Evidently this octopus plays the guitar.
