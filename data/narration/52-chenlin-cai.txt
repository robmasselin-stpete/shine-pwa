When the First Transcontinental Railroad was finished in eighteen sixty-nine, they gathered everyone for the photograph. Everyone except the Chinese laborers who built it. This mural puts them back in the picture.

Three workers stand against the sunset, rendered in blue-gray. Look closely at what each one is doing. One holds a staff. One reads a letter from home. And on the right, one has taken off his hat and knelt to plant a flower — hope, on ground that excluded him. Behind them, the golden peaks are the Sierra Nevada. A sun sets on one side, a crescent moon rises on the other. Aspiration and homesickness, in the same sky.

Chenlin Cai was born in Fujian, China, and holds master's degrees from Tsinghua University in Beijing and the Pennsylvania Academy of Fine Arts.
