This shark once shared screen time with LeBron James. Samsung put it in a commercial. Apple ran it in an iPad ad. Same great white you're looking at right now, fifty-five feet of it, diving headfirst down the east wall of the State Theater.

The artist is David Lew, who goes by Shark Tooof. He trained at the Art Center College of Design in Pasadena, and his hand-drawn great white has turned up on walls from Wynwood to Cambodia. His client list runs from Heineken to Toyota to Axl Rose, who got a personal tour jacket.

He painted this over four days for SHINE, back in twenty fifteen. Roughly one hundred cans of spray paint.

Look at the head. The mouth is wide open, rows of white teeth bared, aimed straight down at that red metal door. The shark means to arrive there.
