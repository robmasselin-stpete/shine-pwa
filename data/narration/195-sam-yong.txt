An Australian flew across an ocean to paint two birds that don't. Ospreys stay close to home, but Sam Yong came from Melbourne for this one, part of St. Pete's SHINE festival in twenty seventeen.

Look at the corner. Two ospreys face each other across it. The one on the left dives down, talons gripping an old brass lantern wrapped in chain — and there's a portrait glowing inside that lantern. The bird on the right rises out of the breaking waves, chain coiling around its body.

Yong is self-taught, trained only in graphic design, and it shows in the control here. Every feather is rendered — brown, white, gray, cream — against a dark navy that makes the white-capped foam and the metal of the chains gleam.
