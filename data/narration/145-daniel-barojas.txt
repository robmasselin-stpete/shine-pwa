This is an offering. Daniel Barojas painted Tlaloc, the Aztec god of water and rain, to protect St. Petersburg from drought and storms — an ofrenda, up on a wall.

That green face with the spiral eyes and fanged mouth is the god himself, crowned in gold. Barojas was born in Mexico and works here in St. Pete, blending ancient mythology with sharp geometric linework and color you can hear from across the street.

He painted this for the SHINE festival in twenty seventeen. The year after, he won the St. Pete Arts Alliance Individual Artist Award and a Creative Pinellas Emerging Artist Award.

Look at the edges. Those crashing ocean waves in deep blue flank both sides of the face, and the whole background is built from interlocking cubes in coral, teal, and brown.
