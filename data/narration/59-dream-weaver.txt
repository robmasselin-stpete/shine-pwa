She painted her first mural at seven, on her aunt's backyard shed in Waco, Texas. She has not stopped.

That's Taylor Smith, who signs her work Dreamweaver — down there in the lower left, in script. This wall is what happens when she gets a big surface and a case of spray cans. It's part flower, part phoenix, exploding outward in neon pink, fiery orange, deep purple, and electric teal against a dark blue field.

Nature runs through everything she paints — birds, water, flora. She works almost entirely in spray paint, and she's left-handed. Somewhere north of a hundred murals, fifteen to twenty of them right here in St. Pete.

She painted this one for the SHINE festival in twenty twenty-two.

Follow the feathered petals out from the center — then find the tropical leaves curling in around the edges to frame the whole thing.
