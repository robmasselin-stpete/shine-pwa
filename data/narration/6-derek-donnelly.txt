That boy in the red cap riding a pelican? That's the artist's son. He shows up in murals all over this city. Derek Donnelly doesn't separate the personal from the public work.

This one's a vintage postcard for the Hollander Hotel — "Greetings from the Hollander Hotel" in classic Florida tourism teals and warm tans. Dolphins leaping, seagulls overhead, and the Sunshine Skyway Bridge way off in the distance.

Donnelly is a Pinellas County native who's painted for the Tampa Bay Rays, Fender Guitars, and, memorably, a T-Rex in a Hawaiian shirt going fishing for AARP. You will see that later on this tour. He also organized a forty-artist tribute mural downtown for a mentor who passed. That instinct to build things for other people keeps showing up.
