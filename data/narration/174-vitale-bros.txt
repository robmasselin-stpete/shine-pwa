That great horned owl in front of you comes from a family business that started on a paper napkin. Nineteen ninety-two, a sketch handed to a restaurant owner. That was the Vitale Bros' first mural.

Johnny and Paul Vitale began hand-lettering signs around Tampa Bay. No formal training. Just artist parents, artist grandparents, and thirty-plus years at it. In the late nineties their warehouse studio hosted the shows that helped birth St. Pete's art scene. They've painted for the Rays, the Bucs, and the set of Spring Breakers. They also call themselves the mural police, restoring damaged walls around the city for free.

They've painted SHINE every year since it began. This one landed for SHINE Origins twenty twenty-five.

Look at the eyes. Piercing orange-amber, staring straight back at you, against that dark military star and its fan of teal and coral stripes.
