That gloved hand reaching toward you? The artist trained in art restoration before he ever picked up a spray can. Andreas von Chrzanowski — he works as Case Maclaim — studied conservation at a German university, then started spraying hands the size of buildings instead.

Hands are his signature. Usually he paints them overlapping and intertwined, a symbol of unity and power. He's done it freehand, no stencils, in more than twenty countries — Detroit, Brooklyn, Miami, Berlin. He painted this one for SHINE in twenty twenty-one, a tribute to healthcare workers.

Everything here is aerosol, worked by hand until it reads like a photograph from clear across the parking lot. Look at the teal scrubs, the surgical cap, the skin.  So much detail to explore.
