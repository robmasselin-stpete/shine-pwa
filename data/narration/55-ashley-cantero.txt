Those three flying saucers aren't just decoration — they're beaming figures right up off the wall, and the whole scene is pure Atomic Age. Teal background, pink and chartreuse, diamond shapes and starbursts, all wrapped in heavy black outlines that make it feel like a vintage comic book page you stepped inside.

Ashley Cantero is a Tampa native who leans hard into nineteen-sixties psychedelia and retro-futurism on purpose. She describes her own work as dark, intricate, sensual, and at times humorous. Over at FloridaRAMA she built an eight-wall retro-futuristic mural and full walk-in dioramas — a drive-in, a swamp motel.

She painted this one for SHINE in twenty twenty-two.

Before you move on, find the suited figure standing on that highway as it shrinks to a single vanishing point. Then look to the right edge — there's a green alien peering back at you.
