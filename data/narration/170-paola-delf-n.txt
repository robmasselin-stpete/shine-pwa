That whole wall is one color story — shades of gray and blue-gray, no other pigment anywhere. Paola Delfín has been working like this since around twenty twelve. Monochromatic women, always tangled in leaves. Her walls run from New Delhi to Berlin to Buenos Aires, and now this one, in the Warehouse Arts District.

She calls it "La Emperatriz" — the empress. The work is about human bonds, intimacy, the things that connect us. Look at the two faces here: the larger woman on the left, crowned in flowers, looking back at you, and a smaller face to the right, half-hidden in the foliage.

The whole thing is painted with the precision of a pencil drawing, just blown up to the size of a building.
