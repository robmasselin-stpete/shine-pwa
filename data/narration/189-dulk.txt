Before Dulk painted this, he went up the Weeki Watch-ee River with the Florida Wildlife Corridor Foundation to see these animals in the wild. Every creature here is researched. The fantastical parts are the flourishes on top of real ecology.

He's from Valencia, Spain, where he grew up copying exotic animals out of his parents' old encyclopedias. He was studying economics — until a friend pulled him into graffiti. Within a year, he dropped economics for illustration.

This mural has two faces. On one, a Florida panther and a wild boar sprint through vegetation under a warm orange sky. Walk around to the other side and it goes underwater, deep teal, a manatee gliding through it.
