That pot-bellied alligator six stories above you? The man who painted it got his start in high school, with a graffiti piece of a Rasta sheep smoking a joint. He's kept the same instincts ever since.

Abys is Julien Brouand, born in France in nineteen ninety-one. His father put a pencil in his hand at eighteen months old. He has no formal art training. What he has instead is a whole cartoon bestiary — jazz-playing rats, thieving raccoons, crabs, and gators like this one. He calls each piece a freeze-frame in full action.

For Shine twenty twenty-four, he crossed the Atlantic and finished this gator in about four days, then painted a bonus crab on the skate shop next door.

Look at the surfboard under the gator's feet. It reads "Shine." And check the hibiscus flower balanced on his head.
