Stand close, and it's a photorealistic portrait — a woman with long locs, every detail sharp. Step back, and the edges dissolve into pure color, warm yellow and maroon bleeding into earth tones. Mwanel Pierre-Louis has built a whole career on that contradiction, the precise subject against the loose wash around it.

He was born in New York to Haitian parents and works out of Miami. His client list is a lineup: Adidas, Jack Daniel's, Starbucks, the NBA, the Miami Dolphins, Formula One Miami. He painted this one for SHINE in twenty twenty-one, on the side of Green Bench Brewing.

The whole piece reads differently depending on where you stand, so try it both ways. Look close and then step back - which do you prefer?
