Back in twenty ten, Chad Myze made a T-shirt that put St. Pete on a list with Paris, Tokyo, and London. Sixteen years later, it's still on the racks here — worn by locals and tourists who mostly agree with the premise.

That civic pride is the whole idea behind what you're looking at. It's called "We Are St. Pete."

Myze moved here in two thousand three and has been pushing the Tampa Bay art scene ever since. He co-founded a Central Avenue gallery that represented more than four hundred artists. He spent a full decade on the organizing committee for SHINE, the mural festival that gave this city a lot of its walls.

His day job clients run from the Dali Museum to the Rays to Coach. His style leans playful, text-driven, tongue-in-cheek.

Take a second and read the words in front of you. He means them...
