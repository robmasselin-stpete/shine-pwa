He quit a corporate job at Oracle to paint walls like this one. Jason Harvin spent years in software before walking away in early twenty twenty-two to paint full-time — and if the crisp edges here look almost machine-made, that's painter's tape, not a projector. Every line laid down by hand.

He grew up in a military family in Germany, studied hospitality in Orlando, then found his real trade in geometric abstraction. During the pandemic he even sold custom accent walls to spruce up people's video calls. This one he painted for SHINE, the city's mural festival.

Take in the whole thing first — the overlapping triangles, the concentric rings, the black-and-white bands cutting through at sharp angles. Then find the center. That big red triangle, ringed in blue and orange, is the eye of the whole storm.
