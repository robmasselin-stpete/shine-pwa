For seven years, Nicole Salgar designed clothes for Ralph Lauren. Now she paints walls three stories tall.

The woman looking back at you is a gatekeeper. Salgar built this piece around the idea of transitions and transformation through our lives, and she stands at the transitions between worlds. 

Salgar trained in tailoring at the Fashion Institute of Technology, then walked away from fashion to make her first mural on Manhattan's Lower East Side. Her work runs on Latin folk art, horror, and old sci-fi cinema. In twenty twenty-five, the Dali Museum picked her for a show.

She painted this one for SHINE, back in twenty twenty-one.
