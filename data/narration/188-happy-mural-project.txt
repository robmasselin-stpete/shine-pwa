Six hundred people painted this wall. Not a crew of professionals — volunteers, over Mother's Day weekend in twenty twenty-four, each taking a thirty-minute slot on a stretch that runs sixteen feet tall and a hundred and five feet long.

It's paint-by-numbers, scaled up past reason. Alyssa Marie designs the wall, breaks it into numbered sections, and hands out brushes. Children take the low parts. The ladder-comfortable handle the top.

Gulf Coast Jewish Family and Community Services commissioned it for their fiftieth anniversary. The building matters too — it once housed the optometry practice of Doctor Philip Benjamin, who fought for affirmative action at the local college. His widow, Marilyn, picked up a brush and helped paint it.
