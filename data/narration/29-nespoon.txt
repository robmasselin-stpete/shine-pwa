The night before NeSpoon was due to land in Florida, Hurricane Milton came ashore near Siesta Key — the strongest storm this region had seen. The festival was postponed. She arrived a month later, found broken branches still in the streets, and decided to put the storm into the wall.

So look at what's happening across this wall. On the left and center, her signature lace — white and pale blue medallions, floral and geometric, shaded so they seem to lift off the concrete like real draped fabric. Then follow it right, and the lace comes undone. Those flowing swirls are Milton's winds, spinning straight out of the threadwork.

NeSpoon has painted in more than forty countries. She researches each city's lace heritage before she lifts a can. Her name is legal, by the way — taken from "there is no spoon" in The Matrix.

Find the unraveling, right of center. That's the hurricane.
