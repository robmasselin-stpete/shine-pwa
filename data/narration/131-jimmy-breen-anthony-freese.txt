The man who designed album art for the Grateful Dead, Green Day, and Bruno Mars also painted this wall. Jimmy Breen and his Wax and Hive partner Anthony Freese made "Lucky You" for the SHINE festival in twenty nineteen. Eighteen feet tall, forty-two feet wide, aimed straight at the interstate so drivers heading north catch it at speed.

It's a grid of good luck charms borrowed from cultures all over the world. A horseshoe pointing up so the luck doesn't spill out. A four-leaf clover. An Italian cornicello, a Japanese beckoning cat, a hamsa hand watching over an evil eye. The premise is simple: we're all lucky to live in Saint Pete.

Breen died in twenty twenty-two, at thirty-seven, one of this city's most prolific muralists.

Find the crossed fingers, tucked into the grid, doing the same job as the horseshoe...
