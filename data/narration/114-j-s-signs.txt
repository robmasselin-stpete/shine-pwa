"Pinellas" is Spanish for "point of pines" — that's the county's original name, and the title of what you're looking at. These three storefronts never existed. Jeffrey Sincich and Josh Stover invented them: a green motel with a neon sign, a mustard post office, a pink apartment with orange columns. Mid-century St. Pete, if you squint, painted in the style of an old children's book.

The two met as ceramics majors at the University of Florida, then founded J&S Signs here in twenty thirteen, painting positive-message murals around town. They've since moved to Portland and taken on Nike, Adidas, and Patagonia. For SHINE twenty eighteen, they came back and painted this.

Look at the motel sign on the left. Under it, a small placard reads "All Are Welcome." Then find the post office at center, still selling oranges for a dollar ninety-nine.
