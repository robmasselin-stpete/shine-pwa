The artist who painted this holds an MFA and a law degree, and once represented the United States at the Dakar Biennale in Senegal. Ya La'Ford designed an IndyCar for Arrow McLaren and was a featured artist for the twenty twenty-one Super Bowl. Here, on the front of an electrical warehouse, she's working in her signature language: sacred geometry and refracted light.

Look at the three white forms against the black wall. Two half-circles on the sides, one full circle centered above the door. Each is packed with angular white linework, straight segments interlocking like shattered ice or stained glass. The black background does the work of night, so the geometry seems to glow.

Look closer at the white network. She's slipped in faint accents of blue, pink, and warm gold, lighting up a few segments inside the crystal.

Her signature sits in the lower right.
