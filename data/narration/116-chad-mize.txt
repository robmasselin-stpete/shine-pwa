That "Paris, Tokyo, London, St. Pete" shirt you keep seeing around town? Chad Mize drew it back in twenty ten, and sixteen years later it's still selling in local shops. This wall is the same instinct, three stories tall — every Florida cliché he could fit, rendered in bold lines and candy colors. Flamingos, a pink dolphin, an orange slice, a mermaid, a retro couple splashing in the surf.

Mize moved here in two thousand three and has been pushing the Tampa Bay art scene ever since. He co-founded a gallery on Central that showed more than four hundred artists, and he sat on the SHINE mural festival committee for a solid decade.

His whole style is tongue-in-cheek, and it's all here. Find the retro couple in their swimwear, mid-splash. They've been having that exact vacation, in that exact surf, this whole time.
