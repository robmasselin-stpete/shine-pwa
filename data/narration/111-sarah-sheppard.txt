Those gears on the right side aren't just decoration. This whole mural is built like a steampunk painting, framed in navy blue with ornate gold trim, and those massive interlocking bronze and gold gears stand for the passage of time and the mechanics of a working life. At the center, a woman becomes a banyan tree. Her body turns into the trunk, her roots spread into the ground, as she reaches out toward a red phoenix on the left — rebirth, quite literally. 

Sarah Sheppard painted this in twenty seventeen for AARP's Age Friendly St. Pete program. She studied metalsmithing to carry on work her late father began. Both her parents were artists, the family trade runs deep.
