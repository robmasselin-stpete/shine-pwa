What you're looking at is a survivor. This slim golden strip is all that's left of their twenty fifteen Shine mural. A white building went up next door in twenty nineteen and swallowed the rest.

That skull grinning at you won a Grammy. Not this mural specifically — but the two men behind it, Doug Cunningham and Jason Noto, took Best Recording Package in twenty twenty-one for a Josh Homme project.

They're Morning Breath, a Brooklyn studio that met at a San Francisco skateboard company back in nineteen ninety-six. Since then, more than a hundred album covers — Foo Fighters, Jay-Z, Eminem, Slayer. They even redesigned a KFC.

Reed what remains. "Warning." "A wicked eyeful." "Signs you need money." The all-seeing eye is still watching. And fittingly, one line reads, "Have a second look." So do.  And if you want a third look Morning Breath have another mural on 9th Street North from 2019...
