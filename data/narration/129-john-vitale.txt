His first mural started on a paper napkin, sketched and handed to a restaurant owner back in nineteen ninety-two. That was Johnny Vitale.

He and his brother Paul built Vitale Brothers in Tampa Bay hand-lettering signs and painting storefront windows. No formal training — just three generations of artists in the family and thirty-some years of practice. Around here they call themselves the mural police, restoring damaged walls across the city for free.

What you're looking at is his solo piece for SHINE Origins twenty twenty-five, painted on the six hundred block where it all began for him. Orange blossoms and ripe citrus climbing the columns of the sage-green building, turning the architecture itself into a Florida grove.
