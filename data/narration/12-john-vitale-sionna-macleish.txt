It started with a napkin. A sketch handed to a restaurant owner in nineteen ninety-two — that was the first Vitale Bros mural. No art school. Just parents who were artists, grandparents who were artists, and thirty-plus years of hand-lettered signs and storefront windows across Tampa Bay.

Johnny Vitale calls himself part of the "mural police," restoring damaged walls around St. Pete for free. After Hurricane Milton, he painted new signs for businesses that lost theirs, no charge. He's painted SHINE every year since it began.

What you're looking at is vintage Florida turned up to full saturation. On the left, a nineteen fifties pin-up with a pink hibiscus, framing the old Thunderbird motel sign. On the right, Sionna MacLeish's roseate spoonbill, soft pink against pale blue polka dots.

Find the bold orange sun on the right panel. That's her signature move.
