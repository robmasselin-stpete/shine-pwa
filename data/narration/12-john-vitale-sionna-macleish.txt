
This wall is two artists, side by side. Johnny Vitale and Sionna MacLeish — Sio — They work closely, and for SHINE Origins they shared a single wall.

The Vitales have made art in Tampa Bay since nineteen ninety-two, when it all started with a sketch handed over on a paper napkin. Signs, storefronts, murals — no art school, just a family that's painted for generations. Johnny has done SHINE every year since it began.

Vintage Florida turned up loud — a nineteen fifties pin-up, a pink hibiscus, the old Thunderbird motel sign.  The other side is quieter: a roseate spoonbill, soft pink against pale blue polka dots.
