That question in the upper right corner — "tell me how you feel" — is doing a lot of work. Below it, a skull and exposed jawbone rise out of tangled green and gray forms on a near-black wall. This is a vanitas, the old Dutch tradition of painting death to make you think about life, blown up to the scale of a building.

Bakpak Durden is a self-taught artist from Detroit, working in the language of Dutch Baroque. He's a Black trans postgender painter whose first solo museum show, at Cranbrook, sold out. He painted this for Shine in twenty twenty-one, the same year he won the Gilda Award.

Look closely at the teeth and the jawbone. The realism is exact, almost photographic — then it dissolves into something organic and strange. Classical technique, asking a very direct question - Are YOU OK?
