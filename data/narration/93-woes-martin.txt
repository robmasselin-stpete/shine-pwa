Those pandas are not having a good day. Snarling, bloodshot, fangs out, claws the size of your arm. This is Woes Martin's signature character, a stressed-out panda that stands for struggle and perseverance. Look closely and you'll notice not every bear is fighting. Some are asleep, one eye cracked open, keeping watch.

Woes grew up on Oahu and taught himself everything through graffiti, hip hop, and comics. His work has landed at Urban Nation in Berlin, at Comic-Con, and on projects for Disney, Converse, and Kidrobot. He painted this wall for SHINE in twenty twenty-one.

The whole pile is black and white against black — except for one thing. Find the bright green bamboo pushing up through the tangle of fur and limbs. In a wall full of exhausted, furious bears, that's the only living thing that looks calm.
