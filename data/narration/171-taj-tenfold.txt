That thread the figure is pulling through the waves is made of water. It's repairing damage to the ocean — a response to sea rise, told through the artist's Jamaican heritage.

Taj Francis calls his style "visual reggae." You may have seen it without knowing: he painted the cover art for John Wick 4, and promotional work for HBO's Lovecraft Country. Marvel and DC are on his client list too. He was born in Saint Andrew parish, Jamaica, and studied illustration in Kingston. For SHINE, in twenty nineteen, he went ninety feet wide and two stories tall right here.

The whole thing runs on jewel-toned Caribbean color — crimson, aqua, royal blue against a light background.
