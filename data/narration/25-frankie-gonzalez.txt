Twenty-five days after hand surgery, Frankie Gonzalez painted Tom Brady. That's the kind of turnaround you get from a man who spent nine years in corporate shipping before he quit the desk and went back to the thing he'd been doing since he was fifteen.

What you're looking at is childhood imagination taking flight, literally. A boy in a baseball cap launches a paper airplane, and watch what happens to his arm — it dissolves from photorealism into bold black brushstrokes and cartoon characters, the whole wall drifting from lush flowers on the left into pure abstraction on the right.

Gonzalez was born in Miami, Danish and Cuban parents, and has painted for ESPN, the Buccaneers, and the Miami Heat.

Look low, along the bottom. Oversized pink peonies, sunflowers, white daisies. Then follow the boy's arm and see where the realism gives out.
