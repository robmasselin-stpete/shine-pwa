George F. Baker the Third once painted a mural live, on air, during ESPN's College GameDay. This whole back wall of Studio@620 is his — every dancing, laughing, selfie-taking character on it.

Look up top. In big bubble letters it reads, "We're all front row at your show." That's the thesis. His work runs on cartoon energy and positive messaging — "Be You," "Be a Light" — pulled from Keith Haring, Nickelodeon, and Dragon Ball Z.

The client list is a brand deck: Nike, Jordan, the NFL, Cartoon Network, Adult Swim. He's also a sneakerhead with more than seventy-eight pairs.

He made this for SHINE in twenty twenty-four. Notice the little white diamonds scattered across the black like confetti, and down in the lower right corner, his signature tucked inside a tiny heart.
