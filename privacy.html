Privacy Policy

Mural Quest — St. Petersburg, FL

Last updated: August 11, 2026

Overview

Mural Quest is a self-guided mural tour app for St. Petersburg, Florida. We respect your privacy and collect minimal data.

Information We Collect

Location Data: The app accesses your device's GPS location to show nearby murals and provide walking directions. Location data is processed on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

Device Orientation: The app uses your device's compass sensors to point you toward murals. This data stays on your device.

No Account Required: We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.

Anonymous Usage Analytics

To understand which murals and features are popular and to improve the app, we collect anonymous, aggregate usage events — for example when the app is opened, which screen or mural is viewed, and search terms entered. These events are tied only to a random, anonymous session identifier generated on your device. They are not linked to your name, email, account, device ID, or advertising ID, and we do not store your IP address or track you across other apps or websites. This data is first-party and self-hosted on our own infrastructure (Cloudflare); it is never sold or shared with advertising networks.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, account credentials, location history, contacts, advertising identifiers, or any information that personally identifies you. We do not sell your data or use third-party ad networks.

Third-Party Services

The app uses OpenStreetMap map tiles to display the map. Your device downloads map tiles directly from tile servers, which may log standard web request information (IP address). See OpenStreetMap's privacy policy.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
